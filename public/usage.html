Members

A print counts toward the reservation it started in, if it started in its first half or up to 15 minutes before it; otherwise toward the reservation it overlaps most. A print more than half outside the member's reservation isn't counted. Cancelled prints count for the share of their estimated time they ran. Prints with no reservation count for a member when staff assign them below.

All-time totals

Every member's usage over all saved data, under the same rules. It updates as date ranges are viewed or recalculated; Recalculate total, on the Admin page, redoes every saved print.

Prints with no reservation

These aren't charged to anyone. If you know whose print it was, type their name under Charge to and press Assign: its filament then counts toward that member's usage. Use the name exactly as Design Hub shows it, so it adds to their other prints. A reservation found later, for example after re-importing the day on the Admin page, takes precedence.
